Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

MidFood delivers food from restaurants in Middelburg, Mpumalanga. To do that we need some information about you. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

MidFood is operated by Sipho Masombuka, Middelburg, Mpumalanga, South Africa. For anything in this policy, or to exercise any of the rights below, contact us at privacy@midfood.co.za.

We are the responsible party for your personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect, and why

WhatWhy we need it
Your name and email address To create your account and let you sign back in.
Your phone number So the driver can call you when they arrive. It is shown to the driver handling your order and to the restaurant preparing it.
Your delivery address So the food reaches you. Shown to the restaurant and the assigned driver.
A location pin (only if you choose to add one) At checkout you can tap Pin my location so the driver can find you without relying on a street address. It is saved with that one order and shown to the driver delivering it. It is never taken unless you tap the button.
Your orders What you ordered, from where, when, and what it cost — so you can see your order history and we can resolve disputes.
Notes you add to an order Passed to the restaurant so they can prepare your food the way you asked.
Notification token (app only) An anonymous identifier from Apple or Google that lets us send you order updates. It does not identify you personally.
Driver location (drivers only) While a driver is online, we collect their location so customers can see their order approaching. It stops when they go offline.

We never see or store your card details. Payments are processed by PayFast, a licensed South African payment provider. You enter your card on PayFast's own secure page. MidFood only receives a yes-or-no answer and a reference number.

Location, specifically

If you are a customer

We do not track your location. You type your delivery address yourself. If you want to, you can also tap Pin my location at checkout: that shares where you are at that moment, once, for that order only, so your driver can find you. We do not follow you afterwards, and you can order without ever using it.

The map on the order-tracking page is drawn with map images from OpenStreetMap. Your browser fetches those images directly from OpenStreetMap, so they receive the same basic technical information any website does (such as your IP address), but nothing about you or your order.

If you are a driver

While you are switched online in the driver app, your device shares its location so that customers waiting on a delivery can see roughly where you are. It stops the moment you switch offline or log out. We keep only your most recent position, not a history of where you have been.

Who we share it with

We share the minimum needed to get your food to you:

  • The restaurant you ordered from — your name, phone number, delivery address, what you ordered and any note you added.
  • The driver delivering your order — the same details, so they can find you and call you on arrival.
  • PayFast — your name, email and the amount, so they can process the payment.
  • Our hosting providers — Render (servers and database) and Axxess (domain and email), who store the data on our behalf.
  • Apple and Google — only the anonymous notification token, to deliver order updates to your phone.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Our servers and database are hosted with Render in the United States. This means your information is transferred outside South Africa. Render provides contractual protections for personal information it processes on our behalf, as POPIA requires for cross-border transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Your account — until you ask us to delete it.
  • Order records — retained after delivery for tax and dispute purposes. South African tax law generally requires business records to be kept for five years.
  • Driver location — only the latest position, overwritten continuously and cleared when the driver goes offline.
  • Notification token — until you uninstall the app or log out.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal information. We may need to keep order and payment records where the law requires it.
  • Object to how we are using your information.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za) if you believe we have handled your information unlawfully.

Email privacy@midfood.co.za and we will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account

Email privacy@midfood.co.za from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will remove your name, email, phone number and saved addresses. Records of completed orders are kept in a form that no longer identifies you, where we are required to keep them.

Children

MidFood is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic between your device and MidFood is encrypted. Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form, so nobody at MidFood can see them. Access to the live database is limited to the people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
